These are described from code only.

Harness artefacts to discount: the game ran at roughly 15 ticks per second, so timed overlays linger. The "Gorath the Breaker" title card over `field-w11-day` and `field-w11-night`, and the pale discs on troops in some shots, come from that. The pale discs do point at a real issue (hit flash is counted down in `draw()`, section 8).

Two bugs found while rendering, both outside pure art but they block looking at the field:

1. Creative mode start leaves the "Preparing your defense…" launch overlay covering the whole window. `js/creative.js:221-231` calls `UI.hideMenusForPlay()` (which shows the overlay by default, `js/ui.js:79`) and nothing hides it afterwards. Confirmed in the harness: the overlay was still `visible` two seconds after start. Fix (S): call `UI.hideMenusForPlay({ showLaunch: false })` there, or hide it after `Game.startCreative()`.
2. Strike screen flashes are close to opaque. `StrikeFX.drawScreenFx` (`js/strike-fx.js:557-563`) replaces each flash colour's alpha with `life/maxLife * 0.85`, so a fireball flash defined as `rgba(255,140,60,0.22)` is drawn at up to 0.85. `ingame-fireball.png` shows the whole canvas as flat orange with the field barely visible. Lightning, heal and rally do the same in white, green and gold, and flashes stack (up to 6). Fix (S): multiply the colour's own alpha by `life/maxLife`.

Facts behind the table:

- Construction state is `ctx.globalAlpha = 0.4 + prog * 0.6` and a progress bar (`js/sprites.js:2842`, `:3172-3187`). No scaffold, no builder at work, no stages.
- There is no damage state. `drawBuildingRaw` never reads `b.hp`. A building at 25% health looks identical to a new one apart from the HP bar drawn by `game.js:14553`.
- Destruction: `finalizeBuildingDestroyed` (`js/game.js:9514`) removes the record, shows a text message and plays the `death` SFX. No rubble, no collapse, no particles beyond the `dust` from the last hit. Buildings with `hp <= 0` are skipped by the draw loop.
- Every hit on a building spawns the same small `dust` puff and a damage number (`js/game.js:5332-5333`).
- The watchtower, mess hall and research lab are the only core buildings with any distinct silhouette. Thirty-eight of the 61 types are one of four label-panel templates.
- Building labels use Cinzel in gold or pale colours; none of the building art uses the bone/blood/black palette the units use. The ink proxy mutes them to brown-grey.

## 2. Terrain and field

Seen in `field-w3-day.png`, `field-w11-day.png`, `ingame-day-buildings-troops.png`:

- **Ground.** One cached canvas (`getBattlefieldCanvas`, `js/sprites.js:2342`): a vertical brown gradient, dark and tan ellipse clumps, a fine speckle, sparse old blood, bones, skulls and cracks, a corner vignette, and a 20px grid at 6% black. It reads as dark brown dirt with a dotted texture. The field is a 400x600 portrait strip in the middle of the screen; on a 1600x1000 window it uses about a third of the width.
- **North edge.** A red band 28px tall with "— FROM THE NORTH —" painted into the ground texture (`js/sprites.js:2545-2549`). The HUD covers most of it.
- **Expansion.** When territory grows, a red tint and a dashed line labelled "— NEW LANDS —" (ground cache) plus a gold dashed line labelled "REALM EXPANSION · LAND n" (`drawTerritoryBorders`, `js/visual-polish.js:388`). Both lines are drawn at the same place; in `field-w11-day.png` the two labels overlap.
- **Obstacles.** Three decoration types are drawn: tree (black trunk, three dark green blobs, ground shadow), rock (small grey facet), barricade (plank fence). Plus neutral den, supply crate ("TP" box) and oil barrel ("!" barrel). `js/procgen-obstacles.js` has no callers; its cluster generator is not wired in.
- **Biomes.** `js/biome-spawn.js` defines plains, forest, mountains, corrupted and hellscape, but they only change spawn weights. The only visual is `drawBiomeRegions` (`js/visual-polish.js:357`): a tinted rectangle with an 8px text label. No biome changes the ground texture, trees, rocks or palette.
- **Hazards.** Five drawers (`js/sprites.js:3231`): plague (green ellipse with dots), fire pit (orange disc), void (purple concentric rings), miasma (grey ellipse with dots), mirror rift (dashed tilted ellipse). In `props-hazards-projectiles.png` they are faint flat ellipses, close to invisible against the ground. They animate on `Date.now()`.
- **Weather.** `pickWeather` (`js/visual-polish.js:105`): rain for the first 400 ticks of every 2400, a storm on every third of those windows, snow for 450 ticks of every 5400, mist from wave 200, ash from wave 1001. Each is a full-field tint plus a thin particle spawn (one streak every 2-5 ticks). Storm lightning is a `Math.random() < 0.004` roll inside the draw call (`js/visual-polish.js:1069`), with a white flash and shake. Weather has no gameplay effect, no ground wetness, no puddles, no sound. Because tick 0 falls in the first window, every run opens in a storm during the first night prep.
- **Lighting.** Day: none. Dusk: a purple tint and an orange band across the top third. Night: a radial blue-black gradient plus a 6% blue wash (`js/visual-polish.js:987-999`). `field-w3-night.png` and `ingame-weather-first-seconds.png` show a flat dark blue field with everything dimmed equally. No light sources: no torches, no lit windows, no glow from mages or fire hazards. Boss on field: a pulsing dark red vignette.
- **Breach line.** Not drawn. Enemies head for `SiegeLoop.breachGoal` and the HUD counts breaches, but nothing on the field marks where the line is. The only related marks are the minimap rally line and the "LINE BREACHED — RALLY!" vignette text.
- **Spawn markers.** At night, three pulsing blood-red chevrons with black outline at each edge the next wave uses, with a black label box ("about 12", unit names) and a skull for a boss (`drawSpawnMarkers`, `js/siege-loop.js:1635`). These are in the right style and read well in `field-w3-night.png`. By day, `drawMultiFrontIndicators` adds edge gradients, lane chevrons, badges and a compass in orange.
- **Ground stains.** Deaths and blasts leave dark red or black ellipses that persist for the run (`SiegeLoop.drawGround`, `js/siege-loop.js:1611`). `stains-and-stuck-hitflash-after-restart.png` shows them accumulating into a readable battle scar. This is the best persistent feedback in the game.
- **Selection and posts.** Selection ring is a dashed yellow circle; move marker a yellow circle-plus; post marker a bone diamond with a dashed line. Path preview and box select are light blue (`rgba(100,200,255)`), a colour used nowhere else in the art.
- **Bars and labels.** Every unit carries an HP bar and, for combat troops, a morale bar; veterans add stars and a gold ring; hunt mode adds a red "H" in a circle; elites add a purple ring and a diamond. In `ingame-zoom-melee-1.png` these overlays cover more area than the figures. Enemy type labels (floating text on spawn) stack on top of each other at the north edge in `field-w11-day.png` and `boss-w10-day.png`.

Observations:

- Strike colours are orange, pale blue, green, gold and purple with soft gradients. None have black outlines. They are the least on-style elements on the field.
- `play()` and `impact()` are called on the same tick as the damage (`js/game.js:10872-10880`, `js/content-expansion.js:1651-1655`), so the fireball and meteor damage, shockwave and flash all happen before the projectile visually arrives.
- Strike life is in `data/strikes.json` `fxLife`; lightning is over in 32 ticks, about half a second.
- No strike leaves a lasting mark except the scorch stain from blast deaths.
- Enemy abilities have very little: firebomb reuses `strikeFire` at half size; hex has only the head mark; wraith wail, war aura and ward draw a ground ring; necromancer and bone summoner summons show "RISE!" text with no effect; enemy siege tower fire looks like an archer.
- No targeting telegraph for enemy area attacks. Boss wind-up is two sprite frames.

## 5. Unit animation

How units are drawn: `drawUnitTopDown` (`js/sprites.js:2139`) picks `drawInkFigure` for humanoids, `drawInkMonster` for six monster shapes and all bosses, `drawSiegeTowerUnit` for the tower. Each (type, rotation, team, frame, scale, state, LOD) is rendered once to a 144px canvas and cached (max 768 entries).

What the sheets show (`units-player-all.png`, `units-enemy-all.png`, `units-anim-a.png`, `units-anim-b.png`):

- A humanoid is a top-down figure: shoulders ellipse, two pauldron discs, a head disc, headgear, one weapon, optional shield, cloak, mount. Heavy black outline. This part is on-style and reads well zoomed in (`ingame-zoom-melee-1.png`).
- Palette is not bone/blood/black. Player units are blue, green, purple and gold with a bright blue team ring; enemies are green or maroon with a red ring. Bosses and monsters are closer to the brief (black mass, bone detail, red eyes).

Faction movement: none. All enemies use the same bob and leg slide. The only per-type motion is the mount "gait" and the monster attack shift table. Undead, goblins, orcs, wargs, wraiths and flyers all move the same way.

### 7.2 Game events with no sound

Checked by reading each handler for a `playSfx`/`SFX` call:

- Day begins (`beginDayPhase`, `game.js:3163`): no dawn cue. The wave-start sound follows later.
- Unit-on-unit melee swing: no weapon sound; only the damage `impact`. `swordHit` is used for buildings.
- Arrow or bolt landing: nothing beyond the generic `impact`. Arrow kills use the default death sound.
- Player soldier dies vs enemy dies: same `deathVar`. No voice, no distinction by family (goblin, orc, undead, monster) except "big".
- Boss death: `deathVar` only, and only "big" if the cause was `fall`. No dedicated cue. "Warlord slain" has no sound.
- General dies: none.
- Summons ("RISE!", `content-expansion.js:1530`): none.
- Spike trap trigger: none found.
- Burrow and emerge: none found.
- Hazards (`FactionHazards.applyToUnit`): none; no ambient fire, bubbling or void hum.
- Weather: rain, snow, wind change, and the storm lightning flash and shake have no sound. No thunder.
- Unit selected, move order, back-to-posts, hunt toggle (`selectUnit`, `moveSelectionToWorld`, `returnSelectionToPosts`, `toggleGlobalHunt`, `setSelectedHunt`): none. Soldiers never acknowledge an order.
- Retreat to med tent, garrison enter/leave, builder repair, construction in progress: none.
- Building damage states and collapse: `death` blip only.
- Silver and bronze stars, general command star (`game.js:5301-5308`): none.
- Ascend unit (`ascendSelectedUnit`): none.
- Achievement unlocked (`achievements.js:217`): none.
- Pause, speed change, quick save: none.
- Early-horn bonus: none specific.
- Breach count reaching the last one before defeat: none specific.
- Pin, stagger, expose, frost, guard: none (`hexCast`, `firebomb`, `shieldBlock`, `arrowThunk` are the only status sounds).
- Dodge: none.
- Scout flare, fortify strikes: no caller found with a sound.
- Flyers (harpy, sky drake, wyrm): no wing sound. Warg riders: no growl. Siege tower rolling: none.
- Faction arrival (first goblin, orc, dark wave): none.
- Kill by fireball or lightning strike on many units: one strike sound; no crowd reaction.

- Night has no music of its own: the siege loop keeps playing at a lower gain under the drone.

- `SFX.victory(themeId)` cosmetic themes are unreachable once the fanfare has loaded.
- SFX gain rises with intensity and is capped at 0.72 (`js/audio.js:835`); there is no ducking of music under a stinger, and no limit on simultaneous stingers (boss roar, banner cue and wave start can overlap).
- Hit sounds have three pitch variants; deaths have one per cause. No per-material variation (flesh, shield, bone, wood, stone).
