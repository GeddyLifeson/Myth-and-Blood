MYTH & BLOOD

Fantasy Tactical Warfare

DIFFICULTY

Balanced tactical defense.

Effective: 100%

Double-click Play Myth and Blood.bat to run as a desktop app — no server needed.

Sound is procedural (no files). Click Begin Defense or anywhere to unlock audio — toggle with 🔊 or V.

TP is awarded once per round when you clear a wave (+8, scaling).

Builder: deploy, select BUILD, click map. Builds outposts, walls, castles, med tents, and mess halls (2 projects at once).

Castle: large compound with 4 walls (2 footman slots each), corner outposts, central Keep, med tent, and mess hall.

General (12 TP): enemies hunt him globally. Station in the Keep to grow a command aura and assign footmen to wall slots inside the compound.

Medical Tent (5 TP): wounded allies below 38% HP retreat here to heal; without a tent they fight to the death.

Mess Hall (5 TP): steady morale boost to troops nearby.

Courier: deploy on field, click a message in the right panel to dispatch to the King.

Spy: one off-field action per wave — steal TP, disrupt, scout, poison.

Difficulty: Baby, Normal, Chad, or Doomslayer — pick before you begin.

Waves never end — each wave brings more, tougher enemies. Siege towers every 5 waves.

Territory: every 10 waves the map expands (+90 wide, +110 deep) — more room to build and defend as you hold the realm.

Multi-front: every 25 waves enemies attack from a new flank (East 25, West 50, South 75) until all four sides assault at once.

Academies: Available from wave 1 — each trains 1 free unit per round when complete. Needs a max-rank (Immortal) mentor of that type on the field. Healer Academy: waves ÷5. General Academy: waves ÷10, requires a promoted Footman. Builder/Courier Academies: only if none on field. TP deploy stays available alongside academy training.

Settlements: Hamlet (100 TP, 5 Builders, 5 waves): +5 TP/round. Merchant Guild (150 TP, 5 Builders): +1 TP/round per guild within hamlet range. Both are large, siegeable economy targets — wall them in.

RTS Escalation (Wave 100+): Enemy hordes swell and settlement raids intensify.

Enemy RTS (Wave 200): Map expands; enemies erect mirror hamlets and guilds (+spawn pressure). TP storage is uncapped.

Hunt mode: soldiers pathfind toward enemies. Click to override with manual move.

Honor names: earn 3 gold stars in your role to be named by the Crown (e.g. Syr Gwyn). Generals keep their name; stars reset on promotion and only boost command aura.
