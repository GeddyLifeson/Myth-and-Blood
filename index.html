Sound is procedural (no files). Click Begin Defense or anywhere to unlock audio — toggle with 🔊 or V.

The Journey — Stage 1 wave defense → Stage 2 empire growth (w100) → Stage 3 kingdom map / Shift+G (w150) → Stage 4 galaxy / Shift+U (w400) → Stage 5 planet conquest (w500). Click LAYERS chips to open unlocked maps.

TP is awarded once per round when you clear a wave (+8, scaling).

Builder: deploy, select BUILD, click map. Starts with basics only — outposts, walls, med tents, mess halls, and a Research Lab (2 projects at once).

Castle (research: Fortification): large compound with 4 walls (2 footman slots each), corner outposts, central Keep, med tent, and mess hall.

General (12 TP): enemies hunt him globally. Station in the Keep to grow a command aura and assign footmen to wall slots inside the compound.

Medical Tent (5 TP): wounded allies below 40% HP retreat here to heal; without a tent they fight to the death.

Enemy symmetry: horde grunts (goblins, orcs) scale slowly — late waves field evil operatives (hellbound legionnaires, grim revenants, juggernauts) that scale like your evolved roster. Promote core troops or deploy operatives of your own.

Survival: you lose when every ally is gone and no academy or barracks remains on the field. Spend TP to deploy troops while you can.

Research Lab (45 TP, 2 Builders): field-analyzes fallen enemies for Science Points — grunts, elites, monsters, and bosses each pay more; gain is capped per wave (extra labs raise the cap). Unlock advanced troops, buildings, academies, settlements, evolved barracks, and eventually the Doomslayer.

Mess Hall (5 TP): steady morale boost to troops nearby.

Courier: deploy on field, click a message in the right panel to dispatch to the King.

Spy: one off-field action per wave — steal TP, disrupt, scout, poison.

Difficulty: Baby, Normal, Chad, or Doomslayer — pick before you begin.

Waves never end — each wave brings more, tougher enemies. Siege towers every 5 waves.

Fixed battlefield: you fight on the starting map only. No territory expansion and no multi-flank map growth — assaults come from the north.

Escalation: later waves bring denser packs, tougher foes, bosses, and horde assaults — pure wave defense pressure.

Academies: Find an Immortal veteran first — promote a matching troop to max rank, deploy them on the field, then found the academy. Each complete hall trains 1 free unit per round. Healer Academy: waves ÷5. General Academy: waves ÷10, needs an honor-cycle Footman. Builder/Courier Academies: only if none on field. TP deploy stays available alongside academy training.

Settlements (optional economy): hamlets and guilds still give TP on your side of the field. Enemies no longer build mirror settlements.

Academy Era (Wave 100+): free academy training comes online and assault pressure ramps hard. Jump in via the Academy Era mode if you want that starting point immediately.

Hunt mode: soldiers pathfind toward enemies. Click to override with manual move.

Honor names & promotions: core troops earn stars from combat — 3 gold stars makes them eligible for a TP promotion (research Veteran Doctrine first). Named veterans do not auto-scale with waves; evolved operatives do. Without TP promotions, footmen and knights fall behind as enemies toughen.

Iron Pit Guild: win on Chad difficulty or higher with a primarily melee army for most waves — pure martial arts dominance, one-on-one spirit.

Bound Spirit Court: win after recruiting three evolved heroes and keeping them on the field together for most waves — teamwork, stands, and bizarre alliances.

North Star Ascetics: win after a lone hero — and only that hero — lands the killing blow on a named warlord. Your fate was sealed before the strike.

Skyburst Order: reach wave 200+, defeat the planetary threat boss, and field a high-ki army when the Worldheart falls — protect the planet.
